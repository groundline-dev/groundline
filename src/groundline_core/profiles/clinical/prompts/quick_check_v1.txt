List only the problems:
- "contradicted": the consultation says something incompatible with the statement (a different dose, side, result, timing or diagnosis; a denial turned into a finding; a hedge made certain).
- "unsupported": the note states something the consultation neither says nor directly implies.

A faithful paraphrase, an abbreviation, a correct unit conversion or a direct clinical summary of what was said is supported: do not list it. Headings and other note structure are not statements.

For each problem give:
- "quote": the words in the note that make the statement, copied exactly (at most 30 words).
- "label": "contradicted" or "unsupported".
- "confidence": a whole number from 0 to 100 for how confident you are that the statement is not supported by the consultation. 50 means you cannot tell. Use the whole range.
- "importance": how much it would matter clinically if the statement were wrong. "critical": a reader could make a wrong clinical decision (diagnosis, medication or dose, allergy, examination finding, test result, plan, safety advice). "supporting": useful context whose error would rarely change care. "peripheral": social detail, pleasantries or administrative content.
- "evidence": for "contradicted", the transcript passage that contradicts the statement, copied word for word (at most 40 words). Otherwise null.

If the note has no problems, reply {"issues": []}.

Reply with only this JSON object, no other text:
{"issues": [{"quote": "...", "label": "contradicted", "confidence": 90, "importance": "critical", "evidence": "..."}]}
